Before adding precise locations or public shops, record the original URL/source, individual fact, retrieval date, licensing/reuse permission, verification method/date and required attribution. Do not treat Google directions results as a licensed data import. Do not scrape or copy proprietary business data without permission. Confirm uncertain category associations independently and update this record.

If OpenStreetMap coordinates or tiles are adopted later, record source objects and retrieval date, comply with ODbL and tile-provider terms and display the required OpenStreetMap attribution wherever map data is shown. No OSM map is displayed in this implementation, so there is no current map attribution claim.

Synthetic shop fixtures are clearly prefixed TEST FIXTURE and only created in the guarded local test database. Integration transactions roll back. Browser tests delete their known fixture IDs in `finally`; no test fixtures belong in production or development seeds.

Typography uses DM Sans and Plus Jakarta Sans, distributed through the version-locked Fontsource packages under SIL Open Font License 1.1. The bundled copyright and license notices are retained in `public/licenses/`; font files are self-hosted. No external photography or decorative hero image is used.

## City guide redesign assets (12 October 2026)

The earlier “no external photography/map data” statement describes the previous implementation. The redesign adds:

- **Pettah neighbourhood photo**: [Sri Lanka - 012 - Market street around Fort Stn & Harbour](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sri_Lanka_-_012_-_Market_street_around_Fort_Stn_%26_Harbour_(1630266755).jpg>), McKay Savage, 15 October 2007, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Commons records the original Flickr source and reviewed license. Original downloaded from the linked Flickr original; resized to 1600px and encoded as WebP (display crops responsive). It is historical neighbourhood context, never labelled as a specific street or shop. Public attribution, source, license and transformations are listed at `/image-credits` and linked beside the photo and in the footer.
- **Category illustrations**: original interface compositions using existing Lucide icons (ISC-licensed package), not product/store photography.
- **Road snapshot**: OpenStreetMap API `https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=79.847,6.932,79.861,6.943`, retrieved 12 October 2026. `src/data/pettah-roads.json` retains highway way IDs, names and ordered coordinates; other object data discarded. The source database is © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This derived snapshot is distributed under ODbL. This license applies to the geographic dataset, not the application code. Attribution is visible below the map and on the credits page.
- **Representative street points**: selected actual middle geometry nodes (not computed bounding-box centres) of name-matched OSM roads in Pettah, stored with source way and name in `src/data/street-locations.json`. 1st Cross Street [10548321](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/10548321), 2nd Cross Street [10548298](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/10548298), Maliban Street [10548310](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/10548310), Prince Street [10548335](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/10548335), Sea Street [34695077](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/34695077), Main Street [625320319](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/625320319). Name/geometry checked against the returned source objects and regional bounds. These are source-validated representative street locations, not field-surveyed entrances or verification of category associations. Database coordinates remain untouched; recorded valid coordinates take precedence where available.

The Leaflet map renders the local vector snapshot. It makes **no tile requests** and therefore has no external tile provider, credentials, paid dependency, bulk prefetch, or public OSM tile-server load. A tile background is deliberately unnecessary for this compact street finder. The source snapshot should be refreshed if roads change; do not claim live mapping. This implementation covers the supplied Pettah streets rather than providing a global basemap.

Precise street photographs and current category/store photographs still need appropriately licensed, individually verified sourcing. Neutral geographic illustrations deliberately fill that gap. No business records, contacts, counts, ratings or availability claims were introduced.
